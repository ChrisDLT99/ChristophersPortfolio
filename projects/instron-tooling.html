Lab Fixtures Designs

R&D Automation • 2025

Overview

My primary objective was to improve the overall laboratory efficiency through the development of specialized test fixtures. The image above highlights a 4-point bend alignment tool, custom Instron attachments, and an adjustable mounting assembly engineered to integrate with the Instron load cell.

Problem

A key challenge in the development of these tools was maintaining tight dimensional tolerances, necessitating several design revisions to accommodate machining constraints and mitigate potential failure during fabrication. For the 4pt bend tool I ultimately decided to use Nylon , this was due to the need for this tool to finish a project by a certain deadline it was also one of the first tools I designed for apple and didnt want to use a metal based material . For the Intron attachments I decided the opposite due to wanting these tools to withstand destructive testing to further understand the fatigue limits and the structual integrity of our new product designs .

Approach

  • Concept exploration
  • Researched Material Properties , Find Similar Tools , Understand Demand
  • Sketched Designs
  • PeerReviews
  • CAD modeling / simulation / FEA
  • Design Review
  • 3D Print Designs
  • Lab Testing
  • Share Discoveries
  • Rapid prototyping and test plan
  • DFM review with vendors

Results & Impact

I designed custom Instron attachments that interfaced directly with the load cell, removing the need for secondary mounting components. Since no standard components existed for these test configurations, the purpose-built tooling substantially increased test efficiency, accuracy, and setup consistency.

Reflection

For future iterations, I intend to increase the number of formal design reviews with team members to leverage peer critique, strengthen design decisions, and elevate overall product quality.
