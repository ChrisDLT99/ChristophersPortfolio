Push'N Cushion — Project

Project Management • 2022

Overview

Me and my Team designed and developed a mechanical seat-lift for elderly and mobility-impaired users, optimizing lift geometry and load distribution to minimize joint stress while enhancing user stability, comfort, and functional independence.

Problem

A major constraint of the project was limited material availability caused by COVID-related supply chain disruptions. This required a complete redesign of the final prototype, transitioning from the originally specified plastic components to a wood-based construction while preserving functional performance. This challenge ultimately strengthened the design by requiring rapid iteration and material-driven problem solving under real-world constraints.

Approach

  • Research
  • Concept exploration
  • CAD modeling / simulation
  • Building Prototype
  • Physical Testing

  • Results & Impact

    While users with mobility limitations responded positively to the product’s performance, feedback highlighted opportunities to reduce our overall size and weight to improve usability and portability. We also werent able to explore other size springs which could of helped with our lift force which aligns with some of the feedback we received when it came to how long it took to fully lift someone out of their seat .

Reflection

In future designs, we aim to implement an intelligent sensing feature that automatically recognizes when the user leaves the seat. We'd need to get a better understanding of electrical parts and posssibly use a raspberry pie which could help with sensor detection . We'd also want to further enhance safety , usability, and user experience so possibly adding sesnsors that could help detect how much the user weighs and it could detect if more or less air needed to come out to lift the individual. Also making it smaller and more portable to be easier to move from various locations would make our seat lift easier to use . The challenge with this would be finding a way to use less materials while also making it portable which could effect the structual integrtity.
